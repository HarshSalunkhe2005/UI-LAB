Questions

Fair things to ask first.

Something missing? Write to hello@relay.example and a human answers.

Does Relay see customer personal data?

Tickets are processed in memory and stored encrypted. On Company, names, emails and card numbers are redacted before anything is analysed. We never train shared models on your tickets.

How is this different from tagging tickets in my help desk?

Tags depend on agents applying them the same way, every time. Relay reads the actual conversation, so it still works for the tickets nobody tagged, which is most of them.

What if our support volume is small?

Relay gets useful at around 200 tickets a month. Below that you probably know the asks by heart, and the free plan is there if you want a second opinion.

Can I change how things are grouped?

Yes. Merge, split or rename any theme and Relay remembers it for next week. Your corrections shape every future digest.

How long does setup take?

About five minutes to connect. Your first digest arrives within the hour, built from the previous 90 days.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes, from the settings page. You keep your exported digests either way.
