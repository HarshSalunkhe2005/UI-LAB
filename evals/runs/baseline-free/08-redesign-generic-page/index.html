01 / Features

Six things we obsess over, so you don't have to.

Orbit is built for the person who gets pinged at 9am asking why revenue dipped.

  • 01

    Answers in milliseconds

    Queries over a billion rows return before you lift your finger off the filter. No loading spinners, no nightly refresh.

  • 02

    Locked down by default

    Encrypted at rest and in transit, row-level permissions, and an audit trail your security team will actually read.

  • 03

    Dashboards that stay tidy

    Drag in a metric, get a clean chart. Share a link and everyone sees the same live numbers, on any screen.

  • 04

    Anomaly explanations

    When a metric breaks pattern, Orbit traces the segments, campaigns and releases behind it and ranks the likely causes.

    Warehouses, ad platforms, billing, CRM. Connect in a few clicks, with no engineering ticket required.

    One shared source of truth

    Comment on a chart, pin a decision, get notified when the number moves. The whole team looks at the same thing.
