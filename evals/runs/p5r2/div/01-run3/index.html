    Questions PMs ask before connecting a help desk.

    Does Relay store our tickets?

    It keeps the text it needs to build the digest and quote customers, encrypted, for as long as your plan's history window. Delete a source and its tickets are removed within 24 hours.

    How does it know two tickets ask for the same thing?

    It compares meaning, not keywords, so "export to excel" and "download as CSV" land together. You can split or merge any group once, and Relay remembers your call next week.

    What about tickets that are bugs or how-to questions?

    They are sorted out of the feature-request list and shown in a separate short section, so a broken login does not hide behind a wish for dark mode.

    How long does setup take?

    About six minutes: pick your help desk, approve read-only access, choose the day. The first digest is built from your last four weeks, so it is useful immediately.

    Can engineers and sales see it too?

    Yes. On Team and Company the digest posts to a Slack channel and has a shareable read-only page. Only PMs on your plan edit groups.
