Questions PMs ask first.

Does Relay send customer data to a model provider?

Tickets are scrubbed of names, emails, phone numbers and card or key patterns before they are read. Scrubbed text is processed under a no-training agreement and deleted after 30 days. The Company plan can run in the EU only.

How is this different from tagging tickets?

Tags are what an agent had time to type. Relay reads the whole ticket and groups by the ask, so a request nobody thought to tag still shows up. You can keep your tags; Relay uses them as a hint.

What if the grouping is wrong?

Split or merge a theme from the digest and Relay remembers the correction for later weeks. Every line links to its tickets, so a wrong group is easy to spot.

How long until the first useful digest?

The first digest arrives the next Monday after you connect, built from the last four weeks of history. Trend arrows begin with the second.

Which helpdesks and languages work?

Zendesk, Intercom, Help Scout and Front. Tickets in English, Spanish, German and French are grouped; other languages appear as “unclear” with a count.

Who else can read the digest?

Anyone you add by email or Slack channel. Account names are hidden by default, and the quote trail respects each reader’s helpdesk permissions.
