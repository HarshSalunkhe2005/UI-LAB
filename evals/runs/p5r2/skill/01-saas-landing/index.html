Relay does not decide what you build, and it does not read private ticket notes or attachments. It cannot see requests customers never write to support, so keep your interviews going.

Questions PMs ask before connecting

Do you store our customers' messages?

Relay keeps the text of closed tickets only for the quotes in your digest, encrypted, and deletes it 90 days after the ticket is closed. Email addresses and names are masked before anything is processed.

How is this different from tagging tickets in our help desk?

Tags depend on whoever replied that day. Relay groups tickets by what the customer wrote, so "bulk roles", "permissions for contractors" and "assign access to many seats" count as one request even if each was tagged differently.

How accurate is the grouping?

Every line links back to its tickets, so you can check it in a click. If two groups are really one, merge them; Relay remembers and applies it from then on. Expect to make a few merges in the first two weeks.

Where does the revenue figure come from?

From Stripe, Salesforce or a CSV of accounts. It is the yearly contract value of accounts that raised the request, counted once per account.

We get 30,000 tickets a month. What happens?

Company reads up to 100,000. Past your limit we keep reading and email you the overage before billing, never silently cut a digest.

Can I cancel?

Yes, from the settings page, any time. Your data export is ready the same minute.
