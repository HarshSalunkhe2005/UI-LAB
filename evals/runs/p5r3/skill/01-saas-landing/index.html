Questions PMs ask first

Do my support agents have to tag tickets?

No. Relay ignores your tags and reads the conversation. If you do tag, we use them as a hint, never as the answer.

How is this different from the AI summary inside my helpdesk?

A helpdesk summarises one ticket. Relay compares all of them across the week, ties them to account value and tells you what moved. It also never merges a bug report into a feature request.

What happens to customer data?

Names and emails are masked before analysis. Tickets are encrypted at rest, never used to train models, and removed within 30 days of you disconnecting or asking us to.

How accurate is the grouping?

Every line shows its tickets so you can check it. When you split or merge a group, Relay applies that choice to future weeks. Most teams stop correcting it by week four.

Does it work for small ticket volumes?

It is useful from about 200 tickets a month. Below that you can read them yourself, and we would rather tell you so.

Can I cancel?

Any time, from the billing page. Monthly plans end at the period you paid for, and you can export every past digest.
