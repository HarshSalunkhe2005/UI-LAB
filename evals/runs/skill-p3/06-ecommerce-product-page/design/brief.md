# Brief (design/brief.md)

1. **Product:** Atlas 38, a 38 L carry-on backpack in recycled sailcloth, from a small travel-goods shop (working brand: Halyard). Ships in 2 days.
2. **Who / where:** A frequent short-haul flyer, mostly on a phone on the commute (375), sometimes desktop (1440), comparing two or three bags and worried about airline size limits, fit and whether the shop is real.
3. **Job:** Decide on colour and torso size, be sure it fits a carry-on sizer, and add it to the bag in under a minute with no surprises on price, shipping or returns.
4. **Mood:** weathered, exact, candid.
5. **Memorable moment:** The whole top of the page is a field of the chosen colour; switch Sail / Harbour / Rust and the ground, the bag and the order lines change together.
6. **Real content:** Headline "Atlas 38". "38 L carry-on backpack in recycled sailcloth." $189.00, ships in 2 days, 30-day returns. Specs: 38 L, 1.1 kg, 55 x 35 x 23 cm, 400 g/m2 recycled sailcloth shell. Reviews 4.6 / 212 with one critical 3-star about harness length.
7. **Constraints:** static single-file HTML/CSS/JS, Google or system fonts, inline SVG/CSS images only, works at 1440 and 375, keyboard and screen reader usable.
8. **Must not feel like:** a generic Shopify theme (white grid, grey placeholder photos, "Most loved" badges, countdown timers, fake scarcity) or an outdoor-brand cliche (topo lines, forest green).

Assumptions (no one to ask): brand name "Halyard", prices of related items, review text, 10-year repair promise and shipping tiers are invented and listed in the handoff.
