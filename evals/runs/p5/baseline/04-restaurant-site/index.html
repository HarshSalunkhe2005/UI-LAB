The story

A net shed, a fire, and no gas line.

Salt & Ember lives in what used to be Tamsin Pryor’s net-mending shed, forty steps from the Gull Quay slip. When Maren and Joss Hallett took the lease in 2021, the first thing they bought was a three-tonne brick hearth. The second was a chainsaw.

There is no gas in the building. Every dish is cooked over split oak and apple wood, on grills raised and lowered by hand, in a kitchen you can see from every one of our twenty-eight seats. Fish comes off the quay in the morning, vegetables from the Penhale allotments, and the salt is scraped from pans two bays along.

We keep the menu short because the fire is honest and the weather is not. If the boats stay in, so does the turbot.

Reservations

Save a seat by the fire.

Private dining

The whole room, and the whole fire.

Closed to everyone else, the Net Shed seats up to 28 around the hearth, or 40 standing. Birthdays, wedding dinners, a very good Monday. We cook a set menu built around your guests and the day’s catch, and the fire is yours until midnight.
