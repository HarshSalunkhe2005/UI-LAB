Will it fit as a carry-on on my airline?

At 55 × 35 × 22 cm, the Atlas 38 meets the size limit of most major carriers when packed normally. Budget airlines with under-seat limits may need it slimmed down. Check your airline’s current rules before you fly.

Is it waterproof?

It is water-resistant with a taped-seam finish, good for heavy rain at a run. It is not submersible. A rain cover pocket is built into the base.
