    Questions PMs ask first

    Does Relay write to my helpdesk or email customers?

    No. Access is read-only on closed tickets. Relay sends one thing, your digest, to the people you list.

    How is this different from the tags my support team already uses?

    Tags record what support decided a ticket was about, and they drift. Relay reads the customer’s own words and groups by what they asked for, then shows you the tickets so you can disagree.

    Can I trust the grouping?

    Each line shows its tickets and you can split or merge a theme; Relay remembers the correction next week. Themes under 8 tickets are marked as thin rather than ranked.

    What happens to customer data?

    Names and emails are masked in the digest. Ticket text is processed to build themes and is not used to train any model. You can delete a workspace and everything derived from it in settings.

    How long until the first useful digest?

    Relay backfills the last eight weeks when you connect, so your first digest already shows trend, not just a snapshot.

    What if we are small and get few tickets?

    Below roughly 100 tickets a week, the weekly digest becomes fortnightly so the numbers mean something. The Pilot plan is enough to find out.
