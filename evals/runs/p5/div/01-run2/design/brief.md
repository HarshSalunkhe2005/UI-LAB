# Brief: Relay landing page
- Audience: PMs at 50-500 person software companies, at their desk Monday morning, skeptical of AI tools, drowning in support-queue noise.
- Job: get a PM to start a 14-day trial (email field, one primary action).
- Mood: dry, trustworthy, editorial.
- Memorable moment: the weekly digest itself as the hero object, with a highlighter mark that draws across the top ask.
- Content: real-sounding digest lines, ticket counts, accounts, ARR at stake. Names/quotes/numbers are invented (disclosed in handoff).
- Constraints: single static file, inline SVG/CSS only, works at 1440 and 375.
- Must not feel like: purple-gradient AI landing, bento grid, glass cards, "Trusted by" logo row, mono-eyebrow cream-serif page.
- Assumption (nobody to ask): pricing tiers and trial terms are invented.
