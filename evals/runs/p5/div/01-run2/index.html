Questions PMs ask first.

Does Relay send our tickets to a model provider that trains on them?

No. Tickets are processed under a no-training agreement, customer email addresses are masked before analysis, and you can delete everything from settings at any time.

How is this different from tagging tickets in our help desk?

Tags depend on agents choosing the right label under pressure. Relay groups by what the customer meant, across phrasings, and joins it to accounts and revenue. Your tags stay as they are.

What if the grouping is wrong?

Merge or split any ask from the digest page. Relay remembers your correction and applies it to next week's tickets.

How long until the first digest?

Relay reads your last 90 days when you connect, so the first digest arrives the next Monday, or immediately as a preview.

Which help desks do you support?

Zendesk, Intercom and Help Scout today. Freshdesk and Front are in progress; email us if you need one sooner.
