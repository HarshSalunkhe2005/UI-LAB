What PMs ask before they connect anything.

Does Relay write to our help desk?

No. The connection is read-only. Relay never creates, edits, tags or closes a ticket, and you can revoke access from your help desk's own settings.

Are our customers' words used to train a model?

No. Names, emails and obvious personal identifiers are removed before analysis, and nothing from your tickets is used to train shared models.

How do I know a count is right?

Every number opens the tickets behind it. If a ticket is in the wrong group, move it, and the count and next week's grouping update.

What if we get few tickets?

Under about 20 a week, grouping is noisy, so Relay sends a flat list instead of pretending to find themes.

Which help desks work today?

Zendesk, Intercom, Help Scout and Freshdesk. Front and Salesforce Service Cloud are in progress; email us if you need one sooner.

Can I cancel?

Any time from the billing page. Yearly plans are refunded pro-rata within 30 days.
