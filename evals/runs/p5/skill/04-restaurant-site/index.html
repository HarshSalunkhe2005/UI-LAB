Mara Pengelly took the old net loft on the quay in 2021 and put a hearth where the winch used to be. There is no gas in the building.

Every plate at Salt & Ember is cooked over oak and apple wood from a coppice six miles inland, split on Mondays when we are shut. The fire is lit at two, is ready by five, and dictates what leaves the kitchen: the langoustines go on the hot end, the leeks sit in the embers until they collapse, the bread goes in last when the heat has dropped.

The boats land at Porth Ennis before light. What the Marnie J and the Girl Kitty don't bring in, we don't cook. The menu is chalked each afternoon, so some dishes below will have moved on by the time you sit down.

Find us

Salt & Ember
The Net Loft, Fish Quay
Porth Ennis, Cornwall TR27 4QL
  • On foot. Ten minutes down the hill from Porth Ennis station. Follow the smell of oak smoke.
  • By car. Quay Road car park, 90 metres away, is pay and display until 8 pm.
  • By boat. Visitor pontoon at the end of the quay; tie up, climb the steps, ring the bell.
  • Call. 01736 000 428, from 2 pm on service days.

Private dining

On Mondays and Tuesdays the hearth is ours, and we will light it for you. The whole room takes up to 28 seated, or a long table of 10 to 12 beside the fire on any service night, after the main room has finished.

A set menu cooked entirely over wood, from £62 a head, with the wine list priced at cost for private bookings. Weddings, wakes, crews coming ashore: we have fed all three.
