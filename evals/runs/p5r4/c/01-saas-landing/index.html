Questions PMs ask before they connect anything.

Does Relay write to our help desk?

No. The connection is read-only. Relay never creates, edits or closes a ticket, and never replies to a customer.

Is our customers' text used to train a model?

No. Names, emails and obvious personal identifiers are removed before analysis, and nothing is used to train shared models.

How do I know a count is right?

Every number links to the tickets behind it. Open the list and check the work. If a ticket is in the wrong group, move it and the digest learns from that.

What if our tickets are messy or in several languages?

That is the normal case. Relay groups by what is being asked, not by keywords, and reads English, Spanish, German, French and Portuguese today.

How soon is the first digest useful?

Immediately. Relay starts with your last 90 days, so the first digest already shows what is rising and what is fading.

Can I cancel?

Any time from the billing page. Yearly plans are refunded pro rata within 30 days.
