Asked before connecting

Does Relay write to our help desk?

No. The connection is read-only. Relay never creates, edits, tags or closes a ticket.

Is our customers' text used to train a model?

No. Names, emails and obvious personal identifiers are removed before analysis, and nothing is used to train shared models.

How do I know a count is right?

Every number links to the tickets behind it. Open the list and check. If a ticket is in the wrong group, move it and the count updates.

What if we have fewer than a few hundred tickets a week?

It still works, but themes with only two or three tickets are shown as "watching", not ranked. Below about 150 tickets a week, a spreadsheet is probably enough.

Which help desks work today?

Zendesk, Intercom, Help Scout and Freshdesk. Others can be fed by a weekly CSV export.

Can I cancel?

Any time from the billing page. Yearly plans are refunded pro rata within 30 days.
