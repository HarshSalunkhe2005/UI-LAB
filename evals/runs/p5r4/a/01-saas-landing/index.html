Questions PMs ask before connecting a help desk.

What does Relay do with ticket data?

It reads ticket text and account IDs, read-only, to build the digest. Customer emails and names are masked before analysis, and you can delete your workspace's data from settings at any time.

How does it tell a feature request from a bug?

Each ticket is classified as an ask, a bug, billing or a how-to question. Anything uncertain goes to a "not sure" list in the digest instead of being quietly counted, and your corrections train your workspace.

Which help desks work with it?

Zendesk, Intercom, Help Scout and Front today. Freshdesk and Jira Service Management are being built. Relay can also read a CSV export if yours isn't listed.

Why count accounts instead of tickets?

Because the most frustrated customer files the most tickets. Counting distinct accounts shows how widespread a need is, and that is the number that should move your roadmap.

How long until the first digest?

Connecting takes around ten minutes. Relay reads the previous eight weeks of tickets on setup, so your first digest already has trends in it.

Can I change what the digest calls things?

Yes. Rename, merge or split any request, and Relay applies it to future weeks. You can also map requests to items in your roadmap tool.
