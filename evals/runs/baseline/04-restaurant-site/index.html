The story

A net shed, a hearth, and a stubborn idea.

Salt & Ember lives in what used to be Tamsin Pascoe's net shed, the last building on the quay before the breakwater. When Mara and Jory Tregear took the lease in 2019 the roof leaked and the floor was shingle. They kept the granite walls, laid a brick hearth where the winch stood, and decided there would be no gas on the premises at all.

That is still the rule. Everything is cooked on one fire, fed with oak from a woodlot up the valley and apple prunings from the orchard behind the church. The hot side sears, the cool side coaxes, and the ash gets used too: leeks buried in it, salt baked in it.

We cook what the Polvane boats bring in, which means the menu changes when the weather does. Twenty-eight seats means we know whose birthday it is.
