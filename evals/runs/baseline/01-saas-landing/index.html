01 / How it works

From a pile of tickets to a page you can act on before standup.

1

Plug in your help desk

Read-only OAuth. Relay pulls the last 90 days of history, strips personal data, and keeps listening. Nobody on support changes how they work.

2

Relay groups what people mean

“Can I export on a timer?” and “why do I copy this CSV every Friday?” land in the same bucket. You can merge, split and rename themes; Relay learns your vocabulary.

3

Monday, one page

Ranked themes with trend, verbatim quotes, accounts and ARR attached. Lands in your inbox or Slack, and links straight to the tickets as evidence.

05 / Questions

The things PMs ask before they ask us.

What happens to our customers' data?

Relay is read-only. Names, emails and obvious personal identifiers are removed before analysis, nothing is used to train shared models, and you can delete your workspace and everything in it with one button. SOC 2 Type II report on request.

How is this different from the tags my support team already uses?

Tags describe what support thinks a ticket is about. Relay reads what the customer actually wrote, and groups by what they want. It also joins to revenue, which tags never do.

Will the themes be right?

Mostly, from day one. You can merge, split and rename any theme in two clicks, and Relay remembers the correction. Every number links back to the tickets so you can check the work.

How long until the first digest?

Connect your help desk, wait about 20 minutes for the backfill, and a digest for the past 90 days is waiting. The recurring one arrives the following Monday.

We use a help desk you don't list.

Tell us. Anything with an API or a CSV export works, and we add integrations in the order people ask for them, which feels right.

Can I cancel?

Any time, from the billing page. Yearly plans are refunded pro-rata within 30 days.
