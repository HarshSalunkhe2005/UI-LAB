# Brief (design/brief.md)

1. **Product:** Relay reads a company's support tickets and sends product managers one weekly digest of what customers are actually asking for, ranked by accounts affected and revenue, with the verbatim tickets attached.
2. **Users:** PMs at 50-500 person software companies, at a desk on a laptop on Monday morning, between standup and planning. Skeptical, time-poor, tired of "AI insights" pitches; they already have a ticket backlog they never read.
3. **Job of the page:** let a PM decide in one scroll whether a ticket-derived digest would beat what they do now, and start a free trial.
4. **Mood:** blunt, warm, evidence-first.
5. **Memorable moment:** the hero shows three raw ticket lines collapsing into one ranked digest row with its counts.
6. **Real content:** Headline: "Your customers already told you what to build." Digest row: "Bulk role assignment - 94 tickets, 31 accounts, $412k ARR, up from 61 last week". Quote and prices in the page (plans: Squad $129, Product org $389, Enterprise custom).
7. **Constraints:** single static HTML/CSS/JS file, Google Fonts or system, inline SVG/CSS only, works at 1440 and 375.
8. **Must not feel like:** a dark purple-gradient AI tool page, a cream-and-serif "tasteful" page, a bento grid, a logo wall of invented companies.
