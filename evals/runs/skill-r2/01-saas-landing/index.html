Questions PMs ask before connecting.

Not here? Write to hello@relay.example. A person answers.

Does Relay write to my helpdesk?

No. Access is read-only. It never replies, tags or closes a ticket.

How does it decide two tickets are the same request?

It compares what the customer is trying to do, not keywords. Every theme shows its member tickets, and you can split or merge themes; Relay remembers your correction next week.

What happens to customer personal data?

Names, emails and phone numbers are removed before analysis, and your tickets are never used to train models for other customers. Enterprise adds a data processing agreement and a regional storage option.

We have only a few hundred tickets a month. Is that enough?

Usually yes. With about 300 tickets a month you get three to five real themes. Under 100, the digest says so instead of inventing patterns.

How is this different from tagging tickets in the helpdesk?

Tags depend on agents applying them the same way every time, and they count tickets rather than accounts. Relay needs no tagging discipline and attaches revenue to each theme.

Can I cancel after the trial?

Yes, from the settings page, with no email required. We take no card during the trial, so there is nothing to cancel by accident.
