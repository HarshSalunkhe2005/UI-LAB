Questions PMs ask before they connect anything

Does Relay write to our help desk?

No. The connection is read-only. Relay never creates, edits or closes a ticket.

Is our customers' text used to train a model?

No. Names, emails and obvious personal identifiers are removed before analysis, and nothing is used to train shared models.

How do I know a count is right?

Every number links to the tickets behind it. Open the list and check the work. If a ticket sits in the wrong theme, move it and the counts update.

How long until the first digest?

Connect your help desk and Relay reads the last 90 days straight away. Most teams see a first digest within the hour.

Does it work in languages other than English?

Yes. Tickets in Spanish, French, German and Portuguese are grouped with their English equivalents, and quotes show the original text.

Can I cancel?

Any time from the billing page. Yearly plans are refunded pro-rata within 30 days.
