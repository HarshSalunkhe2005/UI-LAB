Questions PMs ask before connecting anything

Does Relay write to our help desk or contact customers?

No. It has read-only access and has no way to reply, tag or close a ticket. You can revoke access in your help desk at any time.

How does it tell a feature request from a bug or a billing question?

Each ticket is classified first. Bugs and account issues are kept out of the ranking, and you can see them in a separate section if you want them.

What if two asks are really the same thing?

You can merge or split any group from the digest. Relay remembers, so the same call isn't asked of you next week.

Where do our ticket contents go?

Tickets are processed in the region you pick and are not used to train models. Personal details such as names and emails are masked before grouping, and you can delete everything from settings.

How long until the first digest is useful?

Straight away. Relay reads the previous 90 days when you connect, so your sample digest already has trend lines.
