Questions PMs ask before they connect anything

Does Relay write to our help desk?

No. The connection is read-only. Relay never creates, edits or closes a ticket.

Is our customers' text used to train a model?

No. Names, emails and obvious personal identifiers are removed before analysis, and nothing you send is used to train shared models.

How do I know a count is right?

Every number links to the tickets behind it. Open the list and check the work, or tell Relay a ticket does not belong and the theme updates.

What if we get a few hundred tickets a week, not thousands?

It still works. Themes need about ten tickets to show up, so smaller queues see fewer, sharper items.

Which help desks do you support?

Zendesk, Intercom, Help Scout and Freshdesk today. Others can send tickets by CSV or webhook.

Can I cancel?

Any time from the billing page. Yearly plans are refunded pro rata within 30 days.
