Questions PMs ask before they connect anything.

Does Relay write to our helpdesk?

No. The connection is read-only. Relay cannot reply to, tag, close or edit a ticket.

Is our customers' text used to train a model?

No. Ticket text is processed to build your digest and is not used to train any model. Email addresses and phone numbers are masked before grouping, and you can delete all stored text at any time.

How do I know a count is right?

Open the line. Every request lists the tickets it was counted from. If a ticket does not belong, remove it and the group is recounted the same day.

What if our tickets are not in English?

Tickets in Spanish, French, German, Portuguese and Japanese are grouped together with English ones. Quotes are shown in the original language with a translation beneath.

How long until the first digest?

Relay reads your last 8 weeks when you connect, so the first digest arrives the next morning, with trends already filled in.

Can we cancel?

Yes, from the settings page, at any time. Your plan runs to the end of the month you paid for.
