# Brief
1. **Product:** Relay turns a company's support tickets into a weekly digest of what customers are actually asking for, ranked and quoted, for product managers.
2. **Users:** PMs at 50-500 person software companies, at a laptop between meetings (Monday morning), phone for a glance. Skeptical, tired of dashboards and "AI" pitches; they already sit in Zendesk/Intercom/Help Scout exports.
3. **Job of the page:** let a PM decide in under a minute whether to start a 14-day trial; one primary action: "Get the sample digest" / start trial.
4. **Mood:** dry, editorial, trustworthy (a newsroom desk, not a startup).
5. **Memorable moment:** the hero IS a real digest: a ranked ledger of requests with ticket counts and week-over-week change; click a row to open the actual customer quotes behind it.
6. **Content:** Headline: "Monday morning, you know what customers asked for." Data: 1,284 tickets read last week; 31 requests; top one "Export to CSV from saved views", 94 tickets, up 41%. Pricing: Desk $49/mo, Team $199/mo, Org from $690/mo (invented, flagged as placeholder).
7. **Constraints:** single static HTML/CSS/JS, inline SVG only, 1440 and 375, AA contrast, reduced motion.
8. **Must not feel like:** purple-gradient AI landing page, glowing chat bubbles, sparkles, "supercharge your roadmap", bento grid, dashboard screenshot in a browser frame.
