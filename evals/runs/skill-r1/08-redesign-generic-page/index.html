Six things Orbit does, and what each one saves you

Everything below ships on every plan unless the right-hand note says otherwise.

  • Fast enough to ask twice

    Queries come back while you are still reading the last answer, so asking a follow-up costs nothing and you actually do it.

  • Your data stays yours

  • Dashboards people open

    Build one per team, and change a chart without asking an analyst. Each dashboard opens on what moved since yesterday, not on a wall of totals.
