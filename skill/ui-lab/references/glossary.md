# Glossary: design and web words in plain language

**Pattern.** One reusable piece of interface: a button, a menu, a scroll effect, a whole page section. Packaged patterns live in `patterns/`.

**World.** A complete, consistent style for a page: colours, typefaces, movement, and what it refuses to do. "Editorial quiet luxury" and "departures board" are worlds. Worlds are grouped into families.

**Family.** A group of worlds that share a basic temperament (Paper and Print, Loud and Graphic, Machine and Signal, Instrument and Spec, Soft and Living, Cinematic and Spatial). Gate 2 takes its two candidates from different families.

**Mode.** What a visitor is trying to do: Persuade (decide and act), Operate (finish a task), Read (understand something), Experience (be inside the work).

**Convergent default.** The thing an agent produces when it avoids the obvious slop and lands on the next-most-obvious look. The catalogue is `anti-slop.md`.

**Swap test.** Replace the brand with a competitor's. If nothing in the design needs to change, it is generic.

**Token.** A named value (a colour, a size, a duration) defined once as a CSS custom property and used everywhere.

**Role.** What a colour is for (surface, ink, accent), as opposed to what it looks like (blue).

**Hero.** The first big section of a page, what you see before scrolling.

**Scroll-driven.** Something that changes as you scroll: a picture that turns, a line that draws, a heading that comes into focus.

**Parallax.** Layers that move at different speeds as you scroll, giving a sense of depth.

**Reduced motion.** A device setting that asks sites to tone down movement. Good sites honour it.

**Tabular figures.** Numerals that all have the same width, so columns of numbers line up and live values do not jitter.

**Measure.** The length of a line of text, in characters. 45 to 80 is comfortable.

**Landmark.** A named region of a page (header, nav, main, footer) that assistive technology can jump between.

**Focus ring.** The outline that shows which control keyboard input will act on.

**Skeleton.** A neutral placeholder drawn at the final size while content loads.

**Vertical slice.** Building the most important moment of a page completely before building the rest.

**Direction card.** The one-page record of the choices made at Gate 3: metaphor, fonts, palette by role, tokens, motion, signature moment, refusals.
