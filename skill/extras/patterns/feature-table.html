Built for the question "is this one request or fifty?"

What Relay does and does not do
FeatureWhat it doesWhat it does not do
Request groupingMerges tickets by intent across phrasing, languages and channels.Does not rely on agent tags or macros being applied correctly.
Quote trailEvery line links to the tickets it was counted from, with account size.Never shows a summary you cannot check against the source.
Account weightingSplits counts by plan and seat size so one loud enterprise does not hide in the total.Does not rank by revenue unless you switch that on.
